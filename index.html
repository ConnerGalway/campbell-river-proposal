The Day

Four working blocks, and each one ends with something the participant built.

By 4 pm each participant has a prompt library written for their business, one finished document, a content plan, a custom AI assistant, and a 30-day action list. Open a block to see how each one gets built.

Land acknowledgement, introductions, and an account check so every laptop works before we start. Then the frame for the day: three levels of AI use (do it faster, do it yourself, do what you couldn't before), where most businesses are stuck, and what we are going to build. We skip the hype.

You leave with

Working accounts on two AI tools and a clear picture of which of your tasks belong at which level.

The difference between a generic answer and a useful one is almost always the prompt. We teach one method, Role, Context, Task, Format, and apply it right away to each participant's own work: a review reply, a staff message, a listing description. We also cover the habits that make the output reliable.

You leave with

A personal prompt library of five reusable prompts written for your business.

Each participant picks the one document their business needs and does not have: a WorkSafe or HR policy, an onboarding checklist, a scheduling template, customer reply templates. We work through it from start to finish, including how to check it for accuracy and make it sound like them. This block is built around Erin's WorkSafe example.

You leave with

One complete, usable business document you would hand to staff or a regulator.

Visitors increasingly plan trips by asking ChatGPT and Google where to eat, stay and go. We ask those tools about each participant's business in front of them and fix what we find. Then we build a content system that produces a month of posts from one planning session. For non-tourism businesses the same exercise applies to how clients find and compare them.

You leave with

An AI visibility check with a fix list, and a working content plan for the next month.

Each participant builds a custom assistant loaded with their hours, policies, services, tone of voice and frequently asked questions. By the end it can draft their emails, answer staff questions and brief a new hire. We close with the guardrails every business needs, including what never goes into an AI tool, how to check for errors, and when to tell customers AI was involved.

You leave with

A working custom assistant for your business and a one-page AI guidelines sheet for your team.

Each participant writes a 30-day action list on their personal takeaways page: three tasks to hand to AI next week, one document to finish, and one bigger thing to try. We explain what happens next and how to reach us in between.

You leave with

A personal 30-day AI action list and a takeaways page with every tool, prompt and resource from the day.

Breaks at 10:45 and 2:15, lunch at 12:15. Open to any small business. Examples draw on the visitor economy, but every exercise works for contractors, professional services and non-front-facing businesses. A short survey a week before the day shapes the examples to the room.
